# AI POS Design System

**Version:** 1.0

## Purpose

AI POS is a professional operating system for AI projects.

### Core Principles

-   User First
-   Brand ≠ Semantics
-   Calm, premium interface

## Brand

-   Deep Navy: `#233A8B`
-   Hover: `#1E3175`
-   Accent: `#3B5BDB`
-   Gradient: `#7B5CFF → #2EB8E6`

Brand colors are used only for logo, primary actions and navigation.

## Semantic Colors

### Success

Green only for success, ready, accepted and allowed.

### Warning

Use sand palette instead of bright yellow.

-   Background: `#F8F4EA`
-   Border: `#D8C8A3`
-   Text: `#7A5A1F`

### Error

Use calm terracotta.

-   Background: `#F5E9E6`
-   Border: `#D9A79B`
-   Text: `#8A3F31`

## Surfaces

-   Background: `#F4F6FA`
-   Cards: `#FFFFFF`
-   Dividers: `#E2E6EF`

## Typography

-   Primary: `#1A1F2C`
-   Secondary: `#4A5160`
-   Muted: `#7A8294`

## Cards

White, spacious, minimal shadows.

## Buttons

Primary --- Brand Navy.

Secondary --- White outline.

## Header

Only logo and AI POS. No subtitle.

## Navigation

Thin underline or soft background for active tab.

## Radius

12 px.

## Forbidden

-   Bright yellow
-   Neon
-   Cyberpunk
-   Decorative effects
-   Heavy shadows

## Final Principle

AI POS should feel like a premium professional tool that helps people
work.
